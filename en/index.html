TECHNOLOGY

A financial-grade technical foundation

The architecture is built for high availability, auditability and scalability, so new channels and markets are easy to add.

High Availability & Throughput

Stateless services, multi-active deployment, asynchronous transaction paths, horizontal scaling and canary releases.

Security & Compliance by Design

Encrypted, tiered storage of sensitive data, least-privilege access and full audit logs, designed to PCI DSS requirements.

Channel Adapter Layer

A unified interface abstraction: adding a payment channel means writing one adapter, with no change to upper layers.

Ledger & Reconciliation Engine

Double-entry bookkeeping, idempotent processing and automatic reconciliation make every fund movement traceable.

Real-time Risk Control

Rule engine and list management with real-time blocking by limit, frequency and anomaly.

Open API & Private Deployment

Standard RESTful APIs with request signing, deployable on public cloud, private cloud or on-premise.
